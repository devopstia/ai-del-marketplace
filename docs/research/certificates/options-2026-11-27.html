What the big platforms doCoursera, edX, Udemy, LinkedIn Learning and Pluralsight all lead with the learner's name and the course, show the issuer's logo, a date, a certificate number and a short verification link; Coursera and edX add a signature; LinkedIn Learning adds hours.
What we keep from themName and course as the two biggest things; one signature; ID, date and length in a quiet footer; a QR and a short URL that open the public check page.
What we leave outButtons, links, skill lists, disclaimers and any wording like "certified" or "accredited": it is a certificate of completion.
Signature and verificationEvery design carries the official DevOps Easy Learning seal and "Authorized Representative · DevOps Easy Learning", added automatically to every certificate; no one's handwriting is imitated. The QR opens the existing public check page for that ID (/certificates/<ID>) directly, no login and no typing; the same check works by typing the ID at /certificates/verify ("Verify a certificate" in the site footer).
