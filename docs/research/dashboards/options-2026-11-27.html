Dashboard designs: pick one

Three working prototypes of the DEL Studio dashboard, the richest one, with the same sample data. Change the date range and hover the charts; they respond. The direction you pick becomes one design system for every dashboard: Student, Instructor, DEL Studio, Admin and Super Admin.

Why your dashboard showed $0.00 earningsIt is a reporting bug, not a payments problem: the studio shows instructor share, which is $0 by design on DEL-owned courses. DEL kept $4.55 of your $4.99 sale. Here every DEL view shows gross sales, Stripe fees, refunds and DEL net revenue instead.
What's weak todayFour plain number tiles, one bar chart fixed to 30 days, big empty boxes ("No sales…"), no date filter, no course table, no activity, no completion or quiz data on the dashboard.
What all three addDate ranges (Today to custom), comparison with the previous period, hover details on charts, revenue by course, a sortable course table, student progress, an activity feed, compact reviews and small empty states.

Sample data for the design only: course names are real, every number is made up. Production dashboards show only real records.
